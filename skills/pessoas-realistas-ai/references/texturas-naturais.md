# Texturas naturais

## Positivo (adicionar ao prompt)

```
natural skin texture, visible pores, fine peach fuzz, subtle freckles and moles,
slight skin tone variation, natural under-eye shadows, flyaway hairs,
subtle fabric wrinkles, film grain, shot on 85mm f/1.8, candid
```

## Negative prompt

```
plastic skin, airbrushed, overly smooth, waxy, doll-like, cgi, 3d render, cartoon,
oversharpened, symmetrical perfect face, extra fingers, deformed hands,
text artifacts, watermark, uncanny eyes
```

## Dicas

- Imperfeições leves (poros, assimetria sutil) aumentam o realismo.
- Luz natural suave (janela, golden hour) é melhor que luz de estúdio perfeita.
- Evite "perfect", "flawless", "8k ultra HD" em excesso.
